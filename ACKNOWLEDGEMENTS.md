# Acknowledgements

Signalforge consolidates and adapts work from the following projects. It is an independent repository, not an upstream replacement.

- [ai-automation](https://github.com/typeonce-dev/ai-automation) by Typeonce. Signalforge vendors its OXLint rules and selected guidance.
- [anti-slop](https://github.com/dmmulroy/anti-slop) by Dillon Mulroy. Signalforge vendors its evidence-oriented OXLint rules and shared helpers.
- [slopocop](https://github.com/LBognanni/slopocop) by Loris Bognanni. Signalforge reimplements its custom ESLint rule intent using OXLint.

`anti-slop` and `slopocop` are MIT-licensed. Their copyright notices are retained in [NOTICE](NOTICE). The checked-out `ai-automation` source did not include a licence file when this consolidation was made; its provenance is recorded in [NOTICE](NOTICE). Confirm its upstream licensing before redistributing the copied portions.
